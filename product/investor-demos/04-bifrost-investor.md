# Bifrost — Investor Demo Script

**Target length:** 90 seconds
**Format:** Dashboard screenshot + voiceover
**Audience:** Pre-seed investors
**Focus:** Retention mechanism, expansion revenue

---

## SCRIPT

### Why It Exists (20 sec)

[Slide or founder on camera]

After migration, systems drift apart. The ERP says one thing, the CRM says another, and the warehouse system is three hours behind both. Bifrost prevents that by maintaining real-time semantic synchronization — not batch ETL, not nightly jobs, but event-driven change propagation.

### The Product (30 sec)

[Show the Bifrost dashboard — listeners, event log, conflict panel]

CDC listeners attach to source and target systems. Changes propagate in real-time with semantic mediation — status codes, units of measure, and identifiers are translated between system vocabularies, not just copied.

When systems disagree, deterministic conflict resolution handles it: timestamp-based, priority-based, or escalate to a human. Every event is logged. Every resolution is auditable.

Thirteen pre-built integration playbooks cover SAP, Epicor, NetSuite, Dynamics, Salesforce, and more.

### The Business Case (20 sec)

[Slide]

Bifrost is the retention layer. Once a customer's systems are synchronized through Bifrost, disconnecting means going back to stale data and manual reconciliation. This is the product that makes annual renewals automatic.

It also opens an expansion revenue path: each additional system connection is additional value delivered and an argument for higher-tier subscription pricing.

### Closing (10 sec)

Bifrost turns post-migration maintenance into a product. Real-time sync that customers can't live without.
