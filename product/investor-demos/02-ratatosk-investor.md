# Ratatosk — Investor Demo Script

**Target length:** 2 minutes
**Format:** Deliverable walkthrough with voiceover
**Audience:** Pre-seed investors
**Focus:** GTM wedge, service revenue, pipeline creation

---

## SCRIPT

### Why This Matters to You (30 sec)

[Slide or founder on camera]

Before a manufacturer buys an ERP, they need to understand their data. That's the Ratatosk value proposition — but for investors, the value is different.

Ratatosk is our go-to-market wedge. A $9,500 engagement that requires no commitment to our platform, generates immediate revenue, and creates a qualified pipeline for $35K-$250K/year ERP subscriptions.

Every Ratatosk workshop is a paid discovery call that the customer initiates.

### The Service (45 sec)

[Show deliverable artifacts — taxonomy manifest, coverage report, governance score]

One-day workshop. We ingest the customer's schema — their ERP, their shadow systems, their Access databases and Excel workarounds — and classify every table and column against a 170-table semantic reference model.

The output is machine-readable: a taxonomy manifest, a coverage report showing what percentage of their data we can structurally account for, a conflict summary surfacing where departments disagree on definitions, and an executive briefing.

This isn't a consulting engagement that produces a slide deck. It's a structural analysis that produces artifacts a migration tool can consume directly.

### The Business Case (30 sec)

[Slide: pricing, conversion, credit]

$9,500 remote. $14,500 on-site. Gross margin above 80% — the analysis is tool-driven, not labor-intensive.

50% of the workshop fee credits toward migration services. This creates natural upsell: the workshop reveals the mess, Ragnarok provides the migration path, and Yggdrasil is the destination.

Even if the customer doesn't migrate to Yggdrasil, the workshop is profitable standalone and the governance baseline is valuable with any ERP vendor.

### Closing (10 sec)

Ratatosk is the lowest-friction entry point into a $35K-$250K/year relationship. Paid pipeline, not free trials.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Investors care about:** CAC efficiency, conversion path, gross margin, pipeline mechanics
- **Don't show the operator tool** — show the deliverables and the business model
- **Key stat to emphasize:** 80%+ gross margin on a service that creates ERP pipeline
- **Closing frame:** Ratatosk logo, $9,500 price point, "Paid pipeline, not free trials"
