# Ragnarok — Investor Demo Script

**Target length:** 90 seconds
**Format:** Output walkthrough with voiceover
**Audience:** Pre-seed investors
**Focus:** Revenue expansion from Ratatosk, migration as revenue event

---

## SCRIPT

### The Revenue Event (20 sec)

[Slide or founder on camera]

When a Ratatosk workshop reveals that a manufacturer's legacy data is structurally unsound — and it almost always does — the next question is: "What do we do about it?" Ragnarok is the answer. It turns a $9,500 workshop into a $25,000+ migration engagement.

### What It Does (40 sec)

[Show mapping output, coverage report, migration plan]

Ragnarok connects to the legacy system, maps every column with semantic confidence scores, and produces a phased migration plan sorted by foreign key dependencies.

The key differentiator: human-in-the-loop. Every mapping requires approval before data moves. Every phase validates before advancing. 100% of source data is accounted for — migrate, transform, archive, or explicitly reject. No "we'll deal with it later."

This isn't ETL. It's governed data transition with full coverage guarantees.

### The Business Model (20 sec)

[Slide: pricing and flow]

Migration engagements start at $25,000. Duration depends on source system complexity — typically 2-6 months. The Ratatosk credit ($4,750-$7,250) reduces the effective entry price.

The revenue path: Ratatosk ($9.5K) → Ragnarok ($25K+) → Yggdrasil ($35K-$250K/year recurring). Each step qualifies the next.

### Closing (10 sec)

Ragnarok converts governance insight into migration revenue. It's the middle of the funnel.

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Keep it short.** Ragnarok is a revenue expansion story, not a standalone pitch.
- **The funnel visual is the key takeaway:** Ratatosk → Ragnarok → Yggdrasil with dollar amounts at each step
- **Don't show the operator tool** — show the client-facing migration plan and coverage report
- **Closing frame:** Revenue funnel diagram
