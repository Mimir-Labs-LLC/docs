# Jormungandr — Investor Demo Script

**Target length:** 60 seconds
**Format:** Slide or conceptual diagram with voiceover
**Audience:** Pre-seed investors
**Focus:** Platform completeness, long-term retention

---

## SCRIPT

### The Governance Loop (20 sec)

[Slide: platform lifecycle diagram — Ratatosk → Ragnarok → Bifrost → Jormungandr]

Ratatosk establishes the governance baseline. Ragnarok migrates with semantic fidelity. Bifrost maintains alignment in real-time. But without enforcement, definitions drift back apart over time. Schema changes happen. New columns appear. Synonyms proliferate.

Jormungandr closes the loop.

### What It Does (25 sec)

[Slide or diagram: canon registry, drift detection, compliance alerts]

Jormungandr is a canon governance engine. It maintains an immutable version history of every semantic definition — every table, column, and relationship classification. When a connected system's schema changes, Jormungandr detects the drift and flags it before it compounds.

Continuous validation. Compliance reporting. System-agnostic — it works across any connected platform, not just Yggdrasil ERP.

### Why Investors Should Care (15 sec)

Jormungandr is the reason the platform subscription renews every year. It's the difference between "we migrated your data once" and "we govern your data permanently." Recurring revenue requires recurring value. This is it.

Jormungandr is currently in development. It completes the platform lifecycle.

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Keep it very short.** Jormungandr is in development — don't oversell.
- **The lifecycle diagram is the key visual.** Show the four products as a cycle, with Jormungandr closing the loop back to governance.
- **Honest about status:** "In development" is fine. Investors appreciate a roadmap product that completes the narrative, as long as you don't pretend it's built.
- **Closing frame:** Platform lifecycle diagram with Jormungandr highlighted
