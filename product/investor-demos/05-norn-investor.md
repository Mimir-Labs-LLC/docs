# Norn — Investor Demo Script

**Target length:** 2 minutes
**Format:** Web app screen recording with voiceover
**Audience:** Pre-seed investors
**Focus:** Parallel revenue stream, free tier → paid conversion, product-led growth

---

## SCRIPT

### Why Norn Matters to the Business (30 sec)

[Slide or founder on camera]

Norn is a parallel revenue stream. While Yggdrasil has a 60-120 day sales cycle and $25K+ implementation, Norn has a free tier that converts to $99-$1,499/month SaaS. Different buyer, different cycle, different economics.

It also generates inbound. Every manufacturer who signs up for the free tier is a lead for the broader platform.

### The Product (45 sec)

[Screen recording: import a PDF, detect fields, fill, export]

Import any document — PDF, Word, plain text. Norn auto-detects form fields from the document's content: names, dates, signature lines, checkboxes. One click, and the fields are positioned at their real locations on the interactive PDF canvas.

Fill with a guided form — shared values propagate automatically, formulas calculate live. Send for electronic signature with party-based routing. Export a filled PDF with a tamper-evident audit trail.

This is the workflow that takes 45 minutes with Word and email, compressed to 5 minutes.

### The Business Model (30 sec)

[Slide: tiers and conversion]

Four tiers. Free: 5 contracts/month, no sending. Pro: $99/month, 50 contracts, signature sending. Business: $399/month, 250 contracts, API access. Enterprise: $1,499/month, unlimited.

The pilot is running at $299/month for the Business tier — 25% discount for five qualifying organizations with bi-weekly feedback.

The free tier is the top of the funnel. No credit card, no sales call. A manufacturer imports their first document, sees the field detection work, and the product sells itself. Conversion target: 5-8% free-to-paid within 90 days.

### Closing (15 sec)

Norn is product-led growth for a company that otherwise sells enterprise contracts. It funds the ERP sales cycle, generates leads, and proves the engineering team can ship.

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Investors care about:** PLG mechanics, free-to-paid conversion, parallel revenue, lead generation
- **The "one-click field detection" is the demo moment.** Import a real PDF, click detect, watch 15 fields appear at their correct positions. This is the "wow" that drives free tier signups.
- **Don't spend time on compose mode or sections** — investors don't care about feature depth. They care about the conversion funnel.
- **Key stats:** Free tier → 5-8% conversion, $99-$1,499/mo revenue range, 5-minute workflow vs 45-minute manual
- **Closing frame:** Norn logo, tier pricing, "Product-led growth"
