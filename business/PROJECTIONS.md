# Mimir Labs — 5-Year Financial Projections

*Full Platform: Norn + Ratatosk + Ragnarok + Bifrost + Yggdrasil ERP*

**Updated: April 2026**

## Platform Overview

Mimir Labs generates revenue through five products across two business models:

| Product | Model | Price Range | Sales Motion |
|---------|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Norn** | SaaS (self-serve) | Free – $1,499/mo | Credit card, no sales cycle |
| **Ratatosk** | Services (SOW) | $5,000 – $12,500/engagement | Consultative, 2-4 week cycle |
| **Ragnarok** | Services (SOW) | $15,000 – $75,000/engagement | Consultative, 4-8 week cycle |
| **Bifrost** | SaaS (subscription) | $2,000 – $5,000/mo | Sales-assisted, 30-60 day cycle |
| **Yggdrasil ERP** | SaaS + activation | `$30K + ($10K × Footprint Score)` / yr + $25K – $75K activation | Enterprise sales, 60-120 day cycle |

Additionally:
| Bundle | Price | Contents |
|--------|-------|----------|
| **Ratatosk + Ragnarok Standard** | $20,000 | Governance workshop + migration (up to 50 tables) |
| **Ratatosk + Ragnarok Complex** | $42,000 | On-site governance + migration (50-200 tables) |
| **Ratatosk + Ragnarok Enterprise** | $82,000 | On-site governance + enterprise migration (200+ tables) |

## Risk Factors

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Norn free-to-paid conversion underperforms | Tighten free tier limits; add Pro trial; focus on templates as conversion hook |
| Services don't scale (founder-dependent) | Standardize Ratatosk playbook; hire implementation engineer Year 2; tooling compresses delivery |
| ERP sales cycle too long for pre-seed runway | ERP is Phase 4 — services and Norn fund the business before ERP revenue matters |
| Solo founder risk | First two hires take over core infrastructure; document everything |
| Market doesn't recognize platform value | Lead with individual products; platform story emerges from customer expansion, not from pitch |
| Incumbent response | Incumbents can't replicate Mimisbrunnr without our deployment history; desktop-first architecture is a compliance wedge they can't match |
