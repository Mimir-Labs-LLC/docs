# Consultant Outreach Template — Key Personnel Recruitment

**Purpose.** Reusable outreach templates for recruiting contingent-commitment consultants to named key personnel slots on federal grant applications. Built for SBA E2G (SB-GC7J-26-001), reusable for any future federal application where Mimir Labs needs to fill SME, training, or compliance roles via Letter of Commitment.

**The ask, in one sentence.** Sign a Letter of Commitment to perform a defined role at a defined rate, contingent on the grant being awarded — no work, no contract, no obligation until SBA notifies of award (typically 90–120 days post-submission).

**Substitution markers in the templates below**: `{ROLE}`, `{FTE_DAYS}`, `{DAILY_RATE}`, `{PERIOD}`, `{DEADLINE}`. Fill in before sending.

---

## Variant A — Direct outreach to an individual SME

Use for retired executives, independent consultants, or known SMEs in your professional network. Tight, respectful of time, leads with the specific ask.

**Subject:** Named-personnel slot on SBA cooperative agreement — {ROLE}, contingent commitment

> Dear [First name],
>
> I'm Chris Gaither, founder of Mimir Labs LLC. We build the data platform underneath Yggdrasil ERP — a manufacturing-focused enterprise system for small and mid-market manufacturers. I'm reaching out because we're preparing an application for the SBA Empower to Grow program (SB-GC7J-26-001, $50M, FY26 cycle), and I'd like to name you as **{ROLE}** on the proposal.
>
> The commitment I'm asking for is a one-page Letter of Commitment — contingent on award. No work, no contract, no obligation on your end unless SBA actually awards the grant. If we win, the engagement begins; if we don't, the LOC dissolves with the application.
>
> The shape of the role:
>
> - **Scope**: {ROLE} — [one sentence describing the substantive responsibility]
> - **Time commitment**: approximately {FTE_DAYS} consulting days over a 24–36 month period of performance
> - **Compensation**: ${DAILY_RATE}/day, paid as a contractor (no employment relationship), invoiced against actual days delivered
> - **What I'd need from you for the application**: a current resume (I can send you the federal-grant format, which is shorter and more structured than a job-search CV) and the signed LOC. I draft the LOC; you review and sign.
>
> Why I'm asking you specifically: [one or two specific sentences about the recipient's relevant background — make this real, not generic. If the recipient is unfamiliar, the warm-intro variant below is a better fit].
>
> Application is due {DEADLINE}, so I'd want to have your resume and signed LOC by [deadline minus 10 business days]. Happy to do a 20-minute call this week or next to walk through the role and the timing.
>
> Best,
>
> Chris Gaither
> Founder, Mimir Labs LLC
> [contact info]

## Variant B — Institutional referral request (MEP center, university extension)

Use for institutional contacts where you want either (a) a personnel referral, or (b) a subrecipient relationship in which the institution names the SME and provides them under a subaward.

**Subject:** SBA E2G FY26 application — partnership inquiry / SME referral

> Dear [First name / Director's name],
>
> I'm Chris Gaither, founder of Mimir Labs LLC. Mimir Labs builds the data platform underneath Yggdrasil ERP, a manufacturing-focused enterprise system targeted at small and mid-market manufacturers. We're preparing an application for the SBA Empower to Grow Manufacturing track (SB-GC7J-26-001, $50M, FY26), and I'm writing to explore two possible forms of partnership with [Center / Institution].
>
> **Option 1 — SME referral.** We're staffing key personnel slots that benefit substantially from MEP-affiliated industry depth. If [Center] has consultants in your network who would be open to a contingent-commitment role at the FY26 federal daily-rate equivalent (~$1,180/day cap), I'd appreciate an introduction. The roles in question are {ROLE} and [other role if applicable]. Commitment is by Letter of Commitment, contingent on award — no obligation pre-award.
>
> **Option 2 — Subrecipient relationship.** If [Center] would prefer a more structured arrangement, I'm open to a subaward in which [Center] takes responsibility for delivering a defined portion of the technical-assistance scope and names its own personnel. We'd build the subaward into the budget at a level that reflects the work, and [Center] would handle its own LOC and resume documentation as part of the subaward package.
>
> Either option requires light coordination on our end before the {DEADLINE} submission. A 30-minute call this week or next would be enough to determine which (if either) fits how [Center] typically engages on federal cooperative agreements.
>
> I'd be glad to share the full proposal narrative and budget shell once we're both confirmed under MNDA, if helpful.
>
> Best,
>
> Chris Gaither
> Founder, Mimir Labs LLC
> [contact info]

>

If you're potentially open to it, I'd send you a one-page LOC and the federal-format resume template, and we'd pick this up properly after the application is in.
>

## Letter of Commitment template (what you'll send the recipient to sign)

The LOC is on **the consultant's letterhead** (or, for unaffiliated retired execs, plain personal letterhead). Mimir Labs drafts it for them; they review, sign, and return.

> **[Consultant letterhead]**
>
> [Date]
>
> Christopher Gaither
> Project Director / Principal Investigator
> Mimir Labs LLC
> [Mimir Labs address]
>
> **RE: Letter of Commitment — SBA Empower to Grow (E2G) Manufacturing Program, Solicitation SB-GC7J-26-001**
>
> Dear Mr. Gaither:
>
> I am writing to confirm my commitment to serve as **{ROLE}** on the project proposed by Mimir Labs LLC under solicitation SB-GC7J-26-001, contingent upon award of the cooperative agreement by the U.S. Small Business Administration.
>
> If the award is made, I commit to perform the duties of {ROLE} as described in the application's project narrative and budget justification, at a level of effort of approximately **{FTE_DAYS} consulting days** over the period of performance, at a daily rate of **${DAILY_RATE}**. My compensation will be invoiced as an independent contractor; no employment relationship is created or implied by this letter or by any subsequent contract executed under the awarded cooperative agreement.
>
> I acknowledge that this commitment is contingent upon SBA's decision to fund the proposed project. If the application is not funded, this letter creates no obligation on either party. If the application is funded, Mimir Labs LLC and I will execute a contractor agreement consistent with the terms above and with applicable federal cost principles at 2 CFR Part 200.
>
> I have reviewed the relevant portions of the application's project narrative and budget justification describing my role, and I confirm that they accurately represent the work I will perform.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> [Signature]
>
> [Printed name]
> [Title or "Independent Consultant"]
> [Contact email and phone]

## Sequencing notes

1. **Identify the four roles' candidates in parallel.** Each role has a different best-source channel; running them sequentially loses 4–6 weeks. The Project Director (Chris) is fixed; the other three should be in outreach by the end of week 1.
2. **Budget the LOC turn-around at 7–10 business days from initial email.** Senior consultants often want a call before signing anything; build that into the deadline math.
3. **Never send the LOC blank.** Always send it pre-filled with role, days, and rate. The recipient should be reviewing language, not negotiating from zero.
4. **Track responses in a single sheet.** Even four candidates is enough that you'll lose a thread; keep a column for each role tracking outreach date, response, LOC status, and resume status.
5. **Have a backup for each slot.** If your first-choice Senior Technical Advisor declines, you don't want to be cold-restarting that channel two weeks before submission. Outreach to two or three for each role is normal; you only attach signed LOCs from the ones who say yes.
