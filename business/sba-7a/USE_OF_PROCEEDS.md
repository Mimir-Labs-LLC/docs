## Purpose

Loan proceeds will fund the transition from product development to market entry. All five Mimir Labs products are built and operational. This loan does not fund speculative R&D — it funds working capital, go-to-market execution, and the first hire needed to deliver services engagements and support early customers.

## Category Detail

### First Hire — Implementation/Customer Engineer ($85,000)

12-month salary for a customer-facing engineer who can:
- Deliver Ratatosk governance workshops on-site at customer facilities
- Execute Ragnarok data migrations
- Provide Yggdrasil ERP implementation and onboarding support
- Handle first-line customer success

This hire is the single highest-leverage use of funds. The founder's time is currently split between product development and business development. A second technical resource enables parallel service delivery (generating revenue) while the founder continues platform development and sales.

### Founder Working Capital ($48,000)

12-month salary bridge at $4,000/month. The founder is currently unfunded. This provides subsistence-level compensation that enables full-time focus on the business rather than seeking outside employment to fund operations.
