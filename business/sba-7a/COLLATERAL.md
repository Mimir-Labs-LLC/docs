## SBA Collateral Policy Notes

- SBA does not decline loans solely for lack of collateral if the business otherwise demonstrates repayment ability
- For loans under $500K, SBA Standard Operating Procedure does not require the lender to take a lien on the owner's primary residence if other business assets and cash flow support the loan
- The SBA guarantees 75-85% of the loan, significantly reducing lender exposure
- Software IP can be listed as business collateral via a UCC filing, though its liquidation value is typically discounted 70-90% by lenders
