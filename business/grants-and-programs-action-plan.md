# Grants & Programs — Two-Week Action Plan

*Mimir Labs LLC — Harrisburg, PA. Veteran-founded (USMC), pre-revenue deep-tech (governed ERP + policy/AI substrate), manufacturing + defense-adjacent. In Penn State LaunchBox; MANTEC-partnered; Ben Franklin engagement in progress. Prepared 2026-07-14.*

## The honest read

At pre-revenue, most PA grant programs are a poor fit right now: KIZ pays on *revenue growth*, Manufacturing-PA funds *university researchers with a manufacturer*, CREDC/county are mostly *loans*, and this cycle's marquee deadlines (Ben Franklin Big Idea, Manufacturing PA Innovation) have already passed. The two tracks that are actually built for a pre-revenue veteran deep-tech company — and carry the biggest non-dilutive checks — are **(1) veteran pitch/accelerator grants** and **(2) SBIR/STTR**. Weight the fortnight there; treat the PA-local items as pipeline-building and position-for-later.

## Tier 1 — Do in the next two weeks (open / rolling / highest leverage)

1. **PenFed Foundation — Veteran Entrepreneur Program (VEP), Incubator track.** Veteran-founder accelerator; 2026 runs an **Incubator (early-stage)** and Accelerator, with a **Pittsburgh, PA** cohort. Grant funding via pitch. Perfect stage + veteran + PA fit. **Action:** apply / express interest for the 2026 Incubator now. https://penfedfoundation.org/our-programs/vep/
2. **NSF SBIR/STTR — submit a Project Pitch.** The single largest non-dilutive check at your stage: ~$305K Phase I, up to ~$1.25M+ across phases, no equity. NSF requires a short **Project Pitch** first (rolling; ~3-week response), which gates a full proposal invite. Your deterministic-governance / AI-readiness-substrate story is a strong NSF fit. **Action:** draft + submit a Project Pitch this fortnight. https://seedfund.nsf.gov/
3. **DoD SBIR/STTR.** Defense-adjacent + ITAR-compatible + veteran + prior In-Q-Tel interest make DoD the natural home. Topics release on a BAA cycle (~3×/yr) via DSIP. **Action this fortnight (prerequisites take time):** register in **SAM.gov** and **DSIP** now if not already, and scan current open topics for AI/data-governance / digital-engineering / manufacturing fits. https://www.dodsbirsttr.mil/submissions/
4. **PA SBDC — free SBIR/STTR + grant consulting.** The state's Small Business Development Centers provide *free* SBIR/STTR proposal help — use them to accelerate #2 and #3 at no cost. **Action:** book an intake with your regional SBDC this week.
5. **Second Service Foundation — Military Entrepreneur Challenge.** Nationwide veteran pitch grants ($1K–$15K); low effort, veteran-only field. **Action:** apply for the next event. https://secondservicefoundation.org/mec/
6. **CREDC / Capital Region — local financing.** Non-dilutive *debt*, rolling: Catalyst Loan Fund (disadvantaged / very-small businesses), Enterprise Zone revolving loan (former Harrisburg Technology Corridor). You're squarely in their footprint (Dauphin). **Action:** book a call with CREDC. https://www.harrisburgregionalchamber.org/economic-development/financing-business-growth/

## Tier 2 — Position now for fall / imminent deadlines

7. **Keystone Innovation Zone (KIZ) Tax Credit.** Up to **$100K/yr in *tradable* (sellable) tax credits**; deadline **Dec 1**, awards May 1. The credit = 50% of your gross-revenue *increase*, so pre-revenue it pays ~$0 this cycle — but the play is to **get designated as a KIZ company now** so you can harvest sellable credits the moment revenue arrives. **Action this fortnight:** confirm Mimir's Harrisburg address falls inside a KIZ (Harrisburg University coordinates the Harrisburg KIZ) and start the company designation. Program: https://dced.pa.gov/programs/keystone-innovation-zone-tax-credit-program/ · questions: tsantore@pa.gov / 1-800-379-7448
8. **Ben Franklin (you're already engaged).** Big Idea Contest is *closed* this cycle; TechCelerator runs periodic cohorts. **Action:** with your BFTP contact (Harrisburg office **717-948-6339**), pin down (a) next Big Idea / TechCelerator timing, (b) whether they'll nominate you into DCED/Manufacturing-PA channels, (c) any SBIR match/help. Leverage the relationship you already have. https://cnp.benfranklin.org/
9. **Dauphin County — economic development.** Local Share (gaming) grants + Small Business Loan Program. Local Share skews community/infrastructure, but worth a call to check the current round. https://www.dauphincounty.gov/government/support-services/community-and-economic-development

## Tier 3 — Not this cycle / skip (for transparency)

- **Manufacturing PA Innovation Program** — Jan 2026 deadline passed; it funds a *PA university researcher* partnered with a *manufacturer*. Best played next cycle *through* a Penn State Harrisburg researcher + one of your validation-cohort manufacturers, with Mimir as the tech. Not a direct Mimir application.
- **Ben Franklin Big Idea Contest** — closed; re-opens next year.
- **PA DEP Small Business Advantage Grant** — energy-efficiency / pollution-prevention equipment. Not a fit for software. Skip.

## Suggested sequence (14 days)

- **Days 1–3:** SAM.gov + DSIP registration (unblock DoD SBIR); book PA SBDC intake; email CREDC and your BFTP contact.
- **Days 3–7:** NSF Project Pitch draft (with SBDC); PenFed VEP + Second Service applications (veteran story is quick to tell and you have it).
- **Days 7–14:** Submit NSF Project Pitch; confirm KIZ zone + start designation; scan DoD open topics; CREDC and BFTP calls.

Net: the checks worth chasing at your stage are **NSF/DoD SBIR** (largest, stage-appropriate) and **PenFed VEP** (veteran + PA + early-stage). Everything PA-local is pipeline and position-for-later.
