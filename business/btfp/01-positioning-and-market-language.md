# Positioning & Market Language

*Response to BFTP guidance (J. Harper). The reframe: lead with Business Record Reliability, translate the product names into buyer language, keep the ERP as the long-term destination. Internal working doc — adapt for website, one-pager, email, and opening conversations.*

---

## The category and the wedge

**Category to test:** *Business Record Reliability for manufacturers.*

**One-line positioning:**
> Mimir Labs helps manufacturers find and fix business-record reliability risk before ERP, AI, integration, migration, reporting, or audit initiatives fail downstream.

The lead is **not** "a better ERP." The lead is the upstream diagnostic: *before you invest more, can your business record even support that work?* The ERP is where this ends, not where the conversation starts.

## Buyer-language product map

Translate once, up front, then you may use the branded name. Never open with the Norse name.

| Product | Say this instead | The buyer question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Ratatosk** | Business Record Reliability Diagnostic | Where do our systems, reports, spreadsheets, and workflows disagree? |
| **Mimisbrunnr** | The agreed business dictionary (canonical model) | What do our key business terms actually mean across the company? |
| **Ragnarok** | Controlled migration pathway | How do we move data into a cleaner structure without guessing? |
| **Bifrost** | Governed synchronization layer | How do we keep systems aligned after the cleanup? |
| **Jormungandr** | Governance & drift enforcement | How do we catch systems drifting from the agreed model? |
| **Yggdrasil** | Governance-native ERP / operating platform | What would it look like for governed truth to be the system of record? |
| **ROPE** | Runtime policy enforcement | Can the system check whether an action is allowed *before* it changes the record? |

Rule of thumb: in any first conversation, use the **middle column**. Save the branded names for the second meeting, or for the moment a prospect asks "what do you call it?"

## Sharpen the story — five reframes

| Stop doing this | Do this instead |
|---|---|
| Opening with architecture | Open with the pain: *leadership can't trust whether the official record reflects the real operation.* |
| Leading with Norse product names | Translate once (table above), then speak in buyer language. |
| Framing as "a better ERP" | Lead with Business Record Reliability; let the ERP surface as a *possible* destination. |
| Reporting findings as technical metrics | State what a finding means for **close, audit, migration, integration, production, delivery, or decision confidence.** |
| Sharing internal diagnostic output | Deliver a 2–3 page executive diagnostic a COO/CFO/VP-Ops/controller/CIO can act on. (See `04-executive-diagnostic-template.md`.) |

## Ready-to-use market language

**Short version (website / one-pager / opener):**
> Mimir Labs helps manufacturers find where their business records stop matching the real operation, then proves whether one controlled slice can run with governed records, workflows, reports, and audit trails that tell the same truth.

**Buyer-facing Ratatosk description:**
> Before you invest more in AI, ERP modernization, reporting, automation, or integration, our diagnostic tests whether your systems, spreadsheets, reports, and workflows actually tell the same story about the business.

**Buyer-facing proof-cell description:**
> Instead of asking you to replace your ERP, we choose one operational path and prove whether that slice can run with clearer records, governed state changes, policy enforcement, and audit-ready evidence.

**When the ceiling comes up (where this goes):**
> The diagnostic finds the risk. A proof cell shows one slice running on governed truth. The governance-native platform is where that becomes the system of record — but only after the first two have earned it.

## What this does *not* say

- It does not say "buy an ERP."
- It does not open on architecture, determinism, or policy engines. Those are *how*; the buyer opens on *why* (I can't trust my numbers).
- It does not claim maturity we can't demonstrate. Pair every claim with what's live vs. facilitated vs. roadmap (see `05-product-maturity-snapshot.md`).
