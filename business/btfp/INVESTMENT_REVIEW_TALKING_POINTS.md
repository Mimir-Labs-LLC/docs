# BTFP Investment Package Review — Talking Points

**Mimir Labs LLC**
**April 2026**

---

## 1. Allocation Justifications (anchor to Use of Proceeds)

- **First hire ($85K, 34%)** is the highest-leverage line. Founder time is currently split between product and BD. A customer-facing engineer unlocks parallel service delivery (Ratatosk workshops, Ragnarok migrations) while founder continues sales and platform work. Pays for itself at ~3 Ratatosk engagements.
- **Founder working capital ($48K, 19%)** is subsistence, not salary. $4K/month. Enables full-time focus rather than contracting work to survive. Single cheapest way to keep the founder productive.
- **Sales & marketing ($40K, 16%)** is tied to specific channels: 2–3 manufacturing trade shows, targeted LinkedIn/Google to PA/OH/IN decision-makers, content production. Not a broadcast brand spend.
- **Infrastructure ($22K, 9%)** is already live — VPS, domains, Cloudflare, SendGrid. Not speculative.
- **Legal/compliance ($15K, 6%)** is mostly SOC 2 Type I prep. Gating revenue — enterprise manufacturers won't sign without it.
- **Operating reserve ($12K, 5%)** is deliberate. Lean businesses that skip reserves die in timing gaps.
- **Scaled versions** exist for $150K (tighter, founder salary bridge shortens to 9 months) and $350K (adds SOC 2 audit completion, equipment line).

## 2. Design Partner Access — Ask for the Warm Intros

This is where BFTP has real leverage that money alone does not buy.

- **MANTEC** (South Central PA MEP) — ask explicitly: can BFTP broker a conversation with MANTEC leadership about Ratatosk as a value-add service their members could access?
- **PA manufacturing corridor** — ask for intros to 3–5 qualified prospects in the BFTP portfolio or partner network. Target profile: 50–500 employee discrete/mixed-mode manufacturer running Epicor, Made2Manage, JobBOSS, M1, or similar; recently acquired or planning an acquisition; feeling data migration pain.
- **Clarify "design partner" means paying pilot** — $5K–$12.5K Ratatosk workshop, not free beta. BFTP's role is credibility and access, not subsidy.
- **Customer Advisory Board** — ask if BFTP can help seat a CAB of 3–5 PA manufacturers willing to advise the roadmap in exchange for early access. Classic BFTP play.
- **Other BFTP portfolio synergies** — ask for a portfolio map. Any manufacturing analytics, IoT, quality, supply chain, or M&A integration companies could be integration partners, referral sources, or design partners.

## 3. SBA / SBIR Assistance — Non-Dilutive Capital

- **SBIR Phase I** — ask about PA's SBIR support program (proposal coaching, readiness review). Target agencies: NIST (MEP program overlap), DOD (CMMC-adjacent, manufacturing readiness, Mentor-Protégé), DOE (industrial decarbonization software), DHS (critical infrastructure). Phase I is typically $50K–$275K; non-dilutive.
- **SBA 7(a) preferred lender intros** — we're working on a 7(a) package ($150K–$350K). Ask which PA-based SBA Preferred Lenders BFTP has worked with — warm intro reduces 30–60 days of process.
- **PA DCED programs** — ask what's currently active: Manufacturing Technology Innovation Grants, Apprenticeship Grants, PIDA loans. Some stack with BFTP investment.
- **APEX Accelerator (formerly PTAC)** — help with government contracting. Relevant if SBIR or GSA Schedule is on the horizon.

## 4. Other Things Worth Surfacing

- **Follow-on funding pathway.** Ask which PA-based and PA-friendly VCs BFTP co-invests with (NewSpring Capital, Osage Venture Partners, Robin Hood Ventures, Safeguard Scientifics). Shape milestones to what the next round would require.
- **Professional services intros.** Startup-friendly law firm (MSAs, SOWs, EULAs, IP protection), E&O / D&O insurance broker, accounting/bookkeeping. Already in use-of-proceeds — warm intros cut cost and de-risk.
- **Talent pipeline.** Penn State, Lehigh, Harrisburg U, Shippensburg, York College — ask which programs BFTP has relationships with. First hire may come through co-op or new-grad channel below market rate.
- **CMMC Level 1 self-attestation.** Already in place. DoD supply chain is a massive buyer with specific compliance needs. If BFTP has defense-adjacent portfolio companies, there's a crossover story.
- **Thought leadership as traction signal.** The essay series (*Cost of Convenience*, *What We Take to the Moon*, *Glass Machines*, *What the Builders Know*, *The Greater Fool*) is public. Early engagement from credentialed readers (Jon Reed at diginomica; Peter Winstanley, W3C DCAT editor, reaching out) is evidence the positioning lands with the right audience. Bring metrics: LinkedIn impressions, engaged commenters, inbound inquiries.
- **Products are built.** Five applications, 101 DB tables, 150+ endpoints, 10 weeks from first commit to operational. This is a go-to-market loan, not an R&D bet. Emphasize heavily.

## 5. Risk Framing — Be Ready for the Hard Questions

Don't wait to be asked. Name them first, show the mitigation.

- **Solo founder** → first hire is the largest line item. Architecture is documented. Bus-factor addressed at Year 2 headcount.
- **Pre-revenue** → services revenue (Ratatosk workshops) can begin within 60 days of loan close. Not dependent on SaaS metrics.
- **Long enterprise sales cycle** → Norn SaaS is the self-serve wedge with no approval friction. Services revenue bridges until SaaS ramps.
- **Technology risk** → products are shipped, not shipping. Live demo available.
- **Market competition** → incumbent failure rate is the moat. 56% of ERP implementations fail; every new failure is a potential Mimir customer.
