## 1. Background

[Client] operates under [name the national AI-governance mandate — e.g., the SDAIA AI Adoption Framework / Maroc IA 2030 sovereignty pillar / Abu Dhabi AI-native-government strategy / National Data Policy], which requires AI adoption to be governed, auditable, and accountable. Those requirements depend on the operational systems of record beneath the AI stack producing governed, machine-actionable operational truth. This engagement measures, for one bounded system, how much of [Client]'s operational truth currently lives outside its formal systems — the readiness assessment the mandate requires before AI is scaled on top.

## 2. Scope

A single, bounded diagnostic of **one** system of record / operational domain at [Client]: [name the system — e.g., one ministry workflow, one SOE's procure-to-pay, one plant line].

Ratatosk analyzes, for that bounded scope:
- Database schemas and data models;
- BI / reporting models and their hidden definitions;
- System-to-system integrations and the logic embedded in them;
- Spreadsheets and end-user-computed artifacts acting as shadow systems of record;
- Undocumented rules, states, constraints, exceptions, authority, and approval paths.

Ratatosk is **deterministic and AI-free**, operates **zero-knowledge** (no personally identifiable information is retained), and is **human-in-the-loop authoritative** — the Client's experts confirm every finding. The engagement can run **in-country / on Client-provided infrastructure** to satisfy data-residency requirements.

## 3. Deliverables

1. **Operational-Truth Gap Map** — a structured inventory of where operational meaning, rules, definitions, and dependencies for the bounded scope live *outside* the formal system, with confidence scoring.
2. **AI-Readiness Assessment** — a written assessment of the scope's readiness for governed, auditable AI/agent deployment, mapped to [Client]'s named national mandate, with a prioritized remediation sequence.
3. **Executive readout** — a [60–90] minute findings session with [Client] stakeholders.

Deliverables are the Client's to keep and use internally regardless of any subsequent engagement.
