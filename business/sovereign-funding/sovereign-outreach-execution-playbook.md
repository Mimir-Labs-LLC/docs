## 1. One-page executive summary

**The play.** Sell Mimir Labs into sovereign and government-backed buyers as the **operational substrate for national AI readiness** — not as an ERP. Every priority nation has funded compute, cloud, and models and has issued AI-governance *mandates* (audit, accountability, trust, human oversight). None has named who builds the operational-truth layer those mandates require. That is the wedge.

**The wedge product.** Ratatosk — a 5-to-8-day **paid** National Operational AI Readiness Diagnostic. Low procurement friction, no IP transfer, produces an artifact the buyer's AI authority is mandated to produce anyway. It is the foot in the door for Yggdrasil ERP (substrate), Jormungandr/ROPE (policy enforcement), and Bifrost (inter-enterprise sync).

**Where to go first.** Tier 1: **Saudi Arabia, UAE (Abu Dhabi), Morocco** — codified governance mandates, deep state capital, and clean SOE/industrial pilot surfaces. Tier 2: **Qatar, Egypt, Kenya**. Tier 3 / positioning: **Rwanda** (continental credibility), Oman, Bahrain, Jordan.

**How to enter.** Almost never direct-to-tender. Enter via (a) a government-backed SOE with commercial procurement latitude (OCP in Morocco, ADQ portfolio in UAE, NIDLP/SPARK operators in Saudi), (b) an investment-promotion / landing vehicle (ADIO, Hub71, Invest Qatar, RDB, ADD/CDG), or (c) a development-fund grant rider where budgets are donor-mediated (Rwanda, Kenya, Jordan).

**What to ask for first.** A single paid Ratatosk diagnostic, scoped to one ministry, SOE, or operator, framed against the buyer's own national AI-governance mandate.

**The non-negotiables.** No IP transfer; no source ownership; no exclusivity that blocks US/EU sales; no country capture; no sanctions/export-control exposure (acute around UAE's G42); no defense/intel without legal review; no consulting-without-adoption. Mimir Labs stays originator and lead.

**Why it wins now.** The agentic wave has made the ungoverned-substrate problem urgent and visible; sovereign capital has over-bought the top of the stack; and the governance mandates create a named, funded reason to care that resolves to Mimir Labs' exact mechanism.

Route via an SI or PIF-portfolio modernization program, or a SIDF/Tawteen-funded SOE pilot. Position as complementary to HUMAIN/hyperscaler/SAP, not competitive. Diagnostic first.

- **UAE (Abu Dhabi).** Enter via **ADIO or Hub71** as landing partner with a paid Ratatosk wedge, or **ADQ-SOE direct**. **Do not contract through G42/Core42** in the first deal (export-control contamination risk). Frame against the AD Digital Strategy 2025–2027 AI-native-government targets.
- **Qatar.** Establish a local footprint via QSTP / Startup Qatar (QDB) or Invest Qatar; land a paid diagnostic with one ministry/SOE through the National Centre for AI or National Data Program; use Web Summit Qatar / Qatar Economic Forum for origination.
- **Egypt.** SEZ-first: Bifrost + Yggdrasil across SCZONE tenants, with TSFE as co-investor; or Ratatosk feeding the Center for Responsible AI conformity regime.
- **Kenya.** Diagnostic on a public-sector/SOE system feeding the auditable-decision mandate; fund via AfDB/Smart Africa; scale to Konza-anchored agency.
- **Rwanda.** Direct via **C4IR Rwanda** as governance partner; position Ratatosk as the instrument that operationalizes the AI Policy's public-sector pillar; fund via Gates AI Scaling Hub / World Bank Digital Acceleration. Treat as credibility, not revenue — but contract an explicit Yggdrasil option to avoid the shelf-report trap.
- **Oman / Bahrain / Jordan.** Oman: Madayn industrial-city / OIA-SOE substrate pilot. Bahrain: iGA single-buyer reference deployment. Jordan: diagnostic wedge funded by a development partner via MoDEE/UNIDO.

## 5. Recommended first asks

- **Primary:** one paid Ratatosk **National Operational AI Readiness Diagnostic** (5–8 days), scoped to one ministry, SOE, or operator, framed against the country's own AI-governance mandate.
- **Secondary (where capital-led):** a strategic, non-dilutive or co-investment commitment tied to a named reference deployment (TSFE, OIA, Mumtalakat, SIDF, CDG/FM6I).
- **Never first:** a multi-year platform replacement, an exclusivity arrangement, or an unpaid PoC.

## 6. Outreach sequence

1. **Warm-route mapping (Week 1–2).** For each Tier-1 entity, identify the single best route in (SOE exec, landing vehicle, or summit). Prefer a warm introduction over cold contact.
2. **First contact (Week 2–4).** Concise, mechanism-led message to the policy sponsor *or* SOE buyer (templates in §10). Lead with the governance-mandate hook specific to their country.
3. **Brief delivery (on reply).** Send `sovereign-operational-infrastructure-brief.md` (as a polished PDF), not a deck. Offer a 30-minute call.
4. **Diagnostic scoping call (Week 4–8).** Convert interest into a bounded, paid Ratatosk SOW against one system.
5. **Diagnostic delivery (5–8 days, on contract).** Produce the readiness-gap artifact.
6. **Expansion proposal.** Use the artifact to propose the bounded Yggdrasil ERP substrate (then Bifrost) — evidence funds the next step.

Cadence: first contact → follow-up at day 5–7 → second follow-up at day 14 with a new proof point (a relevant whitepaper, a summit, or a peer-market reference). Three touches, then park and re-approach at the next national event.

**Days 76–90 — Deliver & expand.**
- Deliver the first diagnostic; produce the readiness-gap artifact and a one-page expansion proposal.
- Use the artifact as the reference asset in all open conversations.
- Decide Tier-1 #2 commitment based on evidence.

## 8. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Export-control / sanctions** (acute around UAE G42) | Route first UAE deals via ADIO/Hub71/ADQ-SOE, never G42/Core42. Screen every structure; legal review before any compute/dual-use adjacency. |
| **IP transfer / source-ownership demands** (common in localization & donor deals) | Hard no in contract. Offer in-country deployment + data residency instead of IP. |
| **Exclusivity / country capture** | Decline any market-exclusive arrangement. Mimir Labs remains lead in all markets. |
| **Consulting-without-adoption (shelf report)** — acute in donor-funded markets | Contract the Ratatosk diagnostic with an explicit, priced Yggdrasil option tied to a named workflow. |
| **Hyperscaler / SAP / national-champion incumbency** | Position as the governance substrate *above/feeding* their stack, not a rip-and-replace. |
| **Localization / national-preference procurement** (Morocco 15%, Saudi Tawteen, UAE ICV) | Anchor via SOE/innovation vehicle or local partner; build an ICV-positive, in-country delivery model; localize language. |
| **Founder-time dilution** | Only paid diagnostics, paid pilots, or capital-attached engagements. No innovation-theater. |
| **Defense/intel scope creep** | Legal + export-control review before any such engagement; default decline. |
| **Vendor-scale credibility gap** vs. $100bn national programs | Lead with the diagnostic artifact + mechanism, not company size; secure one named reference fast (Rwanda/OCP) to convert credibility. |

- **180-day:** ≥1 diagnostic delivered with a readiness-gap artifact; ≥1 expansion (Yggdrasil) proposal in procurement; Rwanda/credibility reference initiated; Tier-2 pipeline opened in Qatar/Egypt/Kenya.
