Enterprise Data & AI Readiness — ERP Vendor Radar

Twenty-four functional conditions for implementing and operating enterprise data and enterprise AI, graded 0–20 per index across five incumbent ERP platforms and Yggdrasil ERP (Mimir Labs). Indexes are grouped into four clusters: data architecture & custody, data operations, AI readiness & governance, and operations & economics.

Methodology. Qualitative capability grades on a 0–20 scale, assessed against the functional requirements defined in the Mimir Labs research corpus — The Missing Layer in Enterprise AI Readiness (working paper, May 2026), the ERP structural-failure catalog, and the market analysis. Incumbents are graded on generally available, natively shipped capability (not partner add-ons or services engagements); Yggdrasil is graded on designed and implemented capability and is pre-GA — maturity and ecosystem risk are not encoded in these axes. Scores are directional analyst judgments intended for strategic positioning, not benchmark measurements.
