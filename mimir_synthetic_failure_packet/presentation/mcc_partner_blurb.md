# Mark Cuban Companies — Partner Blurb

*(Under 2,000 characters.)*

---

Mimir Labs builds governance-native enterprise data infrastructure. The thesis: enterprise AI and autonomous agents cannot be reliably governed by wrappers, prompt filters, or workflow screens if the underlying system still accepts semantically invalid business state. Controls are usually bound to a screen or an API, not to the state itself, so any other path that reaches the database is an unenforced write. Agents find those paths and optimize over them.

The answer is enforcement at the write path: business policy, provenance, semantic meaning, and audit invariants checked at the point where state is committed, so invalid transactions are refused before they persist.

Two products, sequenced:

Ratatosk is the diagnostic entry point. It reads a company's existing schemas, exports, and spreadsheets and surfaces semantic conflicts, governance gaps, redundant entities, automation risks, and audit failures. No production access or customer data required. It produces a buyer-ready findings report in days.

Yggdrasil ERP is the long-term governance-native substrate. It enforces canonical meaning and approval/provenance invariants at commit, refusing invalid state across UI, API, bulk import, service accounts, and AI agents alike.

Why this matters for an AI portfolio: every AI company selling into the enterprise builds on operational data that records activity without proving validity, so their agents inherit false facts as ground truth. Mimir Labs is the layer that makes agent output trustworthy, because the substrate underneath refuses to record a business lie.

Preferred partner path: a Ratatosk diagnostic on one portfolio company's operational exports, scoped to a single high-stakes domain (order commitments, supplier traceability, revenue recognition). It produces a concrete failure map and a bounded Yggdrasil ERP pilot target. Low commitment, fast signal.
