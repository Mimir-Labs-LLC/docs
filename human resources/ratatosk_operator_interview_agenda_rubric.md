# Ratatosk Operator Interview Agenda & Evaluation Rubric

## Agenda Overview (75 minutes total)

1. Opening & Role Framing — 10 min
2. Ratatosk Overview — 5 min
3. Comprehension Test — 10 min
4. Scenario Exercise — 20 min
5. Objection Handling — 15 min
6. Closing Question — 10 min
7. Next Steps — 5 min

---

## Evaluation Criteria (Score 1–5)

### 1. Comprehension
Can they understand and restate the concept quickly?

1 — Completely confused
3 — Partial understanding, vague
5 — Clear, concise, accurate

---

### 2. Articulation
Can they explain complex ideas simply?

1 — Rambling / jargon
3 — Understandable but messy
5 — Sharp, clean, minimal

---

### 3. Problem Orientation
Do they seek to understand the situation before pitching?

1 — Jumps to solution immediately
3 — Mix of questions and pitching
5 — Leads with questions, diagnoses first

---

### 4. Signal Detection
Can they identify when a situation is worth pursuing?

1 — Treats everything as opportunity
3 — Some filtering
5 — Strong judgment, filters aggressively

---

### 5. Objection Handling
Can they handle resistance without collapsing or overselling?

1 — Defensive or vague
3 — Basic response
5 — Reframes clearly and calmly

---

### 6. Independence
Can they operate without scripts or constant guidance?

1 — Needs heavy direction
3 — Moderate autonomy
5 — Self-directed, decisive

---

### 7. Commercial Instinct
Do they understand value, urgency, and decision dynamics?

1 — No sense of business impact
3 — General awareness
5 — Connects problem to money, risk, or outcomes

---

## Red Flags (Immediate Elimination)

- Defaults to high-volume outreach thinking
- Cannot restate the problem clearly
- Overuses buzzwords (AI, optimization, transformation) without substance
- Treats every lead as qualified
- Avoids asking questions

---

## Positive Signals

- Asks clarifying questions before answering
- Uses concrete examples (reports, spreadsheets, finance vs ops conflict)
- Understands stakeholder dynamics
- Demonstrates restraint (does not oversell)
- Brings structure to ambiguity

---

## Selection Rule

Select the candidate who:

- Understands the problem fastest
- Filters most aggressively
- Communicates with clarity

Do NOT select based on enthusiasm or activity bias.

---

## Trial Criteria (7 Days)

Each candidate receives:

- 15–20 target accounts
- defined ICP and triggers

Success =

- at least 1 qualified conversation

Qualified means:

- real company
- active or recent problem
- access to stakeholder
- willingness to engage further

---

## Outcome Decision

- 0 qualified conversations → eliminate
- 1 qualified conversation → continue
- 2+ qualified conversations → prioritize

Focus on signal quality, not volume.
