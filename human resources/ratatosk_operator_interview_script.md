# Ratatosk Operator Interview Script

## Opening (0–5 min)

“Thanks everyone for joining.

This is not a traditional interview. I’m not looking for volume outreach or calendar setting. I’m looking for someone who can understand a problem quickly, recognize when it exists in the real world, and engage intelligently enough to qualify it.

This role is performance-based. There is no base compensation. You are paid for qualified opportunities and closed work. Today is part of the evaluation process and is not compensated.

If that model doesn’t work for you, no hard feelings—this probably isn’t the right fit.”

---

## Framing the Role (5–10 min)

“What you would actually be doing:

- Identifying companies dealing with ERP, integration, or reporting issues
- Engaging them in a way that surfaces whether the problem is real
- Qualifying before bringing anything to me

You are not sending 1,000 messages. You are finding signal.

If you bring me noise, this doesn’t work. If you bring me one real situation, it does.”

---

## Ratatosk Explanation (10–15 min)

Deliver your concise explanation of Ratatosk (keep it tight and consistent).

Example structure:
- What problem exists (semantic conflict, hidden data meaning issues)
- Why current discovery fails (guesswork, interviews, misalignment)
- What Ratatosk does (maps schema + stakeholder meaning into a deterministic blueprint)
- What the output is (action plan + machine-readable manifest)

---

## Comprehension Test (15–25 min)

“Explain what I just said in one sentence.”

Go one by one.

Do not help. Do not clarify.

---

## Scenario Exercise (25–45 min)

Prompt:

“A $150M manufacturer just implemented an ERP. Their reports don’t match reality. Finance doesn’t trust the numbers. Operations is using spreadsheets. Where do you start the conversation?”

Let candidates respond.

Follow-up prompts if needed:
- “What would you ask first?”
- “Who do you talk to?”
- “How do you know if this is worth pursuing?”

---

## Objection Handling (45–60 min)

Prompt:

“We already have consultants for that.”

Each candidate responds.

Optional follow-up:
- “Why would they need anything different?”

---

## Close (60–70 min)

“Why would you succeed at this when others wouldn’t?”

Listen for ownership and clarity.

---

## Final Instructions (70–75 min)

“Next step is a 7-day trial.

You’ll be given a small set of target accounts.

Your goal is not volume—it’s to produce at least one qualified conversation.

Whoever demonstrates the ability to generate real signal continues. Others don’t.

I’m not optimizing for activity. I’m optimizing for outcomes.”
