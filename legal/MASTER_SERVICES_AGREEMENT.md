## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, Provider offers data platform services including data governance analysis, data migration, system integration, schema governance enforcement, and enterprise resource planning software; and

**WHEREAS**, Client desires to engage Provider for one or more of these services under a unified contractual framework;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

**1.1** "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.

**1.2** "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to business data, technical data, trade secrets, pricing, customer lists, and product roadmaps.

**1.3** "Deliverables" means the work product, reports, artifacts, software configurations, and other materials produced by Provider under a Statement of Work.

**1.4** "Intellectual Property" means all patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, and other proprietary rights.

**1.5** "Services" means the professional services, software services, and related activities described in one or more Statements of Work executed under this Agreement.

**1.6** "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a document executed by both Parties that describes the specific Services, Deliverables, timeline, and fees for a particular engagement. Each SOW is governed by and incorporated into this Agreement.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — SCOPE AND STRUCTURE

**2.1 Master Agreement.** This Agreement establishes the general terms and conditions governing all Services provided by Provider to Client. Specific engagements are defined in individual Statements of Work.

**2.2 Order of Precedence.** In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and a Statement of Work, the Statement of Work shall control with respect to the specific engagement, except that this Agreement shall control with respect to limitations of liability, indemnification, confidentiality, and governing law.

**2.3 Additional Agreements.** Certain Services may require additional agreements, including but not limited to a Data Processing Agreement, SaaS Subscription Agreement, or Service Level Agreement. Such agreements are supplementary to this Agreement and shall be referenced in the applicable SOW.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — SERVICES

**3.1 Available Services.** Provider offers the following service categories, any combination of which may be engaged via SOW:

**(a) Ratatosk — Data Governance Workshop.** Structured, facilitated governance discovery producing semantic manifests, conflict analyses, data quality assessments, and executive-ready governance artifacts. Delivered as a time-bounded professional service engagement.

**(b) Ragnarok — Data Migration.** Structured data migration from legacy enterprise systems to target platforms, including schema mapping, data transformation, integrity validation, and post-migration verification. Delivered as a project-based professional service.

**(c) Bifrost — Live Data Bridge.** Deployment, configuration, and ongoing support of persistent, event-driven synchronization between enterprise systems. May include software licensing, deployment services, and managed support.

**(d) Jormungandr — Canon Governance.** Deployment and management of continuous schema governance enforcement, including canonical registry establishment, drift detection configuration, and compliance reporting. May include software licensing and managed monitoring.

**(e) Yggdrasil ERP — Platform Subscription.** Multi-tenant enterprise resource planning platform delivered as a hosted software service, governed by a separate SaaS Subscription Agreement referenced in the SOW.

**(f) Custom Services.** Additional professional services as described in a SOW, including but not limited to consulting, training, custom integration development, and data quality remediation.

**3.2 Performance Standard.** Provider shall perform all Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with generally accepted industry standards.

**3.3 Personnel.** Provider shall assign qualified personnel to perform the Services. Provider may, in its reasonable discretion, replace personnel provided the replacement possesses equivalent qualifications.

**3.4 Client Cooperation.** Client shall provide Provider with timely access to personnel, data, systems, facilities, and information reasonably required for Provider to perform the Services. Delays caused by Client may result in timeline extensions and additional fees as specified in the applicable SOW.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Fees.** Client shall pay the fees specified in each SOW. Fees may be structured as fixed-price, time-and-materials, recurring subscription, or milestone-based, as defined in the SOW.

**4.2 Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Client in accordance with the payment schedule specified in the SOW. If no schedule is specified, Provider shall invoice monthly in arrears.

**4.3 Payment Terms.** All invoices are due and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Late payments shall accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

**4.4 Taxes.** All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. Client is responsible for all sales, use, VAT, and similar taxes, excluding taxes based on Provider's income.

**4.5 Expenses.** Reasonable travel and out-of-pocket expenses incurred by Provider in connection with on-site Services shall be reimbursed by Client, provided such expenses are pre-approved in writing or specified in the SOW.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**5.1 Provider IP.** Provider retains all Intellectual Property rights in its pre-existing technology, tools, methodologies, frameworks, software, and know-how, including but not limited to the Ratatosk, Ragnarok, Bifrost, Jormungandr, and Yggdrasil platforms, the Mimisbrunnr semantic model, and all system dictionaries (collectively, "Provider IP"). Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of Provider IP to Client.

**5.2 Client IP.** Client retains all Intellectual Property rights in its pre-existing data, business processes, and proprietary information ("Client IP").

**5.3 Deliverables.** Ownership of Deliverables shall be as specified in the applicable SOW. Unless otherwise specified:

**(a)** Governance artifacts produced by Ratatosk (manifests, reports, visualizations) are owned by Client upon full payment.

**(b)** Migration plans and mapping configurations produced by Ragnarok are owned by Client upon full payment.

**(c)** Bifrost routing configurations and sync rules created specifically for Client are owned by Client upon full payment.

**(d)** Jormungandr canonical registries and governance rules created specifically for Client are owned by Client upon full payment.

**(e)** Provider retains a royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use anonymized, de-identified insights from engagement work for product improvement and internal benchmarking.

**5.4 No Competing Use.** Provider shall not use Client-specific Deliverables to perform services for Client's direct competitors without Client's prior written consent.

## ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Obligations.** Each Party shall: (a) hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose Confidential Information to third parties except as permitted herein; and (c) use Confidential Information only for the purpose of performing or receiving Services under this Agreement.

**6.2 Permitted Disclosures.** A Party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, contractors, and advisors who have a need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than those herein.

**6.3 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

**6.4 Compelled Disclosure.** If required by law or legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the receiving Party shall promptly notify the disclosing Party (to the extent permitted by law) and cooperate in seeking protective measures.

**6.5 Duration.** Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years, except that obligations regarding trade secrets shall survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## ARTICLE 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION

**9.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with this Article. Individual SOWs have their own terms as specified therein.

**9.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon ninety (90) days' written notice, provided that all active SOWs shall continue to be governed by this Agreement until their completion or termination.

**9.3 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party: (a) commits a material breach that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice; (b) becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or has a receiver appointed; or (c) ceases to conduct business.

**9.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination: (a) Client shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination; (b) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information; and (c) Provider shall cooperate in transitioning Services as reasonably requested by Client.

**9.5 Survival.** Articles 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, and 12 shall survive termination.

**10.2 By Client.** Client shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Provider from and against any third-party claims arising from: (a) Client Data, including claims that Client Data infringes a third party's rights; (b) Client's use of Services in violation of this Agreement; or (c) Client's breach of Applicable Data Protection Law.
