### 1. Purpose of Disclosure
The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship, partnership, transaction, technical integration, or collaboration (the "Authorized Purpose"). In connection with the Authorized Purpose, each party (as a "Disclosing Party") may disclose to the other (as a "Receiving Party") certain confidential and proprietary information.

### 2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means all non-public, proprietary, or sensitive information disclosed by a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party, in any form (written, oral, electronic, or visual), that is marked or identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It includes, without limitation, technical data, source code, architectures, methodologies, product and business plans, financial information, pricing, and customer, supplier, and personnel information.

### 3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was rightfully known to it without restriction before disclosure; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of or reference to the Confidential Information. A Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or court order, provided it gives prompt written notice (where legally permitted) and reasonable cooperation so the Disclosing Party may seek protective treatment.

### 4. Obligations of Confidentiality
Each Receiving Party agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence, to use it solely for the Authorized Purpose, and to protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of like importance (and no less than a reasonable degree of care). Access shall be limited to those representatives with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement, and each party remains responsible for its representatives' compliance.

### 5. Intellectual Property
Each party retains all right, title, and interest in and to its own Confidential Information and intellectual property. No license or other right is granted under any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret, except the limited right to use the Confidential Information for the Authorized Purpose. Any joint development or transfer of rights shall be governed by a separate, subsequent written agreement.

### 6. Return or Destruction
Upon the Disclosing Party's written request or the termination of the parties' discussions, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and any materials derived from it, except that it may retain one archival copy solely for legal-compliance purposes and any copies stored in routine, automated backup systems, which remain subject to this Agreement.

### 7. Term and Termination
This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and remains in effect for three (3) years. Either party may terminate the parties' evaluation at any time upon thirty (30) days' written notice. The confidentiality obligations in this Agreement survive termination and continue for three (3) years from the date of disclosure; obligations with respect to trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

### 8. No Warranty; No Obligation
All Confidential Information is provided "as is," without any warranty of accuracy or completeness. Nothing in this Agreement obligates either party to disclose any information, to proceed with any transaction, or to enter into any further agreement.

### 9. Remedies
Each party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and that the non-breaching party is entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

### 10. General
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the confidential treatment of information disclosed for the Authorized Purpose and supersedes all prior understandings on that subject. It may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties, and may not be assigned without the other party's prior written consent. It is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and which together constitute one and the same instrument.
