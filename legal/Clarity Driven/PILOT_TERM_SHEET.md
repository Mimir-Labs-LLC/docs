### 1. What This Is

Mimir Labs licenses its Schema Workshop tool to Clarity Driven for use within a single named educational course or programme. The license is scoped to a pilot period, after which both parties evaluate and either convert to a commercial license or wind down.

### 2. Pilot Terms

| Term | Value |
|------|-------|
| Pilot duration | 90 days from Effective Date |
| Scope | One named course or programme (to be specified at signing) |
| Maximum participants | 30 per cohort during pilot |
| Number of cohorts | Up to 2 during pilot period |
| Pilot fee | $0 (goodwill / evaluation basis) |
| Post-pilot conversion | Required — see Section 6 |

### 3. What Clarity Driven Gets

- Access to the Schema Workshop web application for use within the named course
- The right to guide participants through the tool as part of a facilitated workshop or self-paced module
- A co-branded version with "Powered by Mimir Labs" attribution
- Reasonable technical support during the pilot (email, async)

### 4. What Clarity Driven Does Not Get

- Ownership of the tool, its source code, its logic, or its output structure
- The right to sublicense, resell, redistribute, or white-label the tool
- The right to use the tool outside the named course without a separate agreement
- The right to modify, reverse-engineer, or create derivative works from the tool
- The right to represent the tool as a Clarity Driven product

### 5. Branding and Attribution

- The tool will display "Powered by Mimir Labs" in the interface at all times
- Course materials referencing the tool must attribute it as "Schema Workshop by Mimir Labs"
- Clarity Driven may describe the tool in course marketing but may not imply ownership or co-development
- Mimir Labs retains the right to reference the course as a case study (with Clarity Driven's approval on specific language)

### 6. Post-Pilot Conversion

At the end of the 90-day pilot, the parties will negotiate one of the following commercial structures:

| Model | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Per-cohort fee | Fixed fee per course cohort using the tool |
| Per-participant fee | Fee per enrolled participant who accesses the tool |
| Annual license | Flat annual fee for unlimited cohorts within the named course |

If the parties do not reach a commercial agreement within 30 days of pilot expiration, the license terminates and Clarity Driven ceases all use of the tool.

### 7. Lead Referral

If a course participant wants to apply the Schema Workshop or any Mimir Labs product commercially — inside their own company, for a client, or for any purpose beyond the educational exercise — that inquiry routes to Mimir Labs.

Clarity Driven agrees to:
- Direct such inquiries to Mimir Labs (via a provided contact or referral link)
- Not independently offer commercial schema design services using the tool
- Not represent the ability to deliver Mimir Labs products or services

Mimir Labs agrees to:
- Pay a referral fee of 10% of the first-year contract value for any referred lead that converts to a paid Mimir Labs engagement
- Acknowledge the referral source to Clarity Driven within 30 days of conversion

### 8. Testimonials and Case Study

If the pilot is successful, both parties agree to discuss (but are not obligated to provide):
- A written testimonial from Clarity Driven for Mimir Labs marketing use
- A written testimonial from Mimir Labs for Clarity Driven course marketing
- A co-authored case study describing the educational use case and outcomes

### 9. Termination

Either party may terminate the pilot at any time with 14 days written notice. Upon termination:
- Clarity Driven ceases use of the tool in all courses
- Any in-progress cohort may complete their current module (grace period: 30 days)
- No refunds apply (pilot is $0)

### 10. Next Steps

This term sheet is non-binding and is intended to align both parties before drafting the formal Educational Use License Agreement. If these terms are acceptable:

1. Both parties sign this term sheet to indicate alignment
2. Mimir Labs drafts the formal license agreement based on these terms
3. Clarity Driven specifies the named course and target cohort dates
4. Mimir Labs provisions the co-branded tool instance
