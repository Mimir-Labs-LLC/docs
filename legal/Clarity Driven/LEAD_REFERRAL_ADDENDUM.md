# Lead Referral Addendum

**Addendum to Educational Use License Agreement**
**Between Mimir Labs LLC and Clarity Driven**

---

This Addendum supplements Section 6 of the Educational Use License Agreement ("Agreement") dated _______________ and provides operational detail for lead referral between the Parties.

---

## 1. What Constitutes a Referral

A "Qualified Referral" is an introduction from Licensee to Licensor where:
- (a) The referred party is a Named Course Participant, or was introduced to Mimir Labs through the Named Course or Licensee's network;
- (b) The referred party expresses interest in a Commercial Deployment (as defined in the Agreement);
- (c) The referral is communicated to Licensor via the designated contact method before or within 14 days of the referred party's first contact with Licensor.

## 2. Referral Process

**Step 1 — Identification.** When a Participant or contact expresses interest in using Schema Workshop, Ratatosk, Ragnarok, Bifrost, Yggdrasil, or any Mimir Labs product commercially, Licensee introduces them to Licensor via:
- Email to doc@mimirlabs.net with subject line "Referral: [Company Name]"
- Include: contact name, email, company, and a brief description of the interest

**Step 2 — Acknowledgment.** Licensor acknowledges receipt within 5 business days and assigns a referral tracking ID.

**Step 3 — Engagement.** Licensor engages directly with the referred party. Licensee is not expected to participate in sales conversations unless invited.

**Step 4 — Conversion.** If the referred party signs a paid agreement with Licensor within 12 months of the referral date, the referral is considered "Converted."

**Step 5 — Payment.** Licensor pays the referral fee within 30 days of receiving first payment from the referred client.

## 3. Referral Fee Structure

| Engagement Type | Referral Fee |
|----------------|-------------|
| Ratatosk Workshop | 10% of engagement value |
| Ragnarok Migration | 10% of first engagement value |
| Bifrost Integration (monthly) | 10% of first 12 months |
| Yggdrasil ERP (subscription) | 10% of first 12 months |
| Norn (subscription) | 10% of first 12 months |
| Schema Workshop (commercial license) | 10% of first-year license fee |
| Bundle / custom engagement | 10% of first-year contract value |

## 4. Referral Window

A referral is valid for 12 months from the date it is submitted. If the referred party does not convert within 12 months, the referral expires and no fee is owed. If the referred party re-engages after the window, Licensee may re-submit the referral.

## 5. Exclusions

No referral fee is owed for:
- (a) Leads that Licensor was already in contact with prior to the referral (Licensor will notify Licensee within 5 business days if this applies);
- (b) Leads generated through Licensor's own marketing, website, or inbound channels where no Licensee introduction occurred;
- (c) Internal use by Licensee (Licensee using Mimir Labs products for its own business is not a referral).

## 6. Reporting

Upon request (no more than quarterly), Licensor will provide Licensee a summary of:
- Referrals received and their current status (open, converted, expired, excluded)
- Fees earned and paid

## 7. Non-Circumvention

**7.1** Licensee shall not circumvent Licensor by directly offering, quoting, or delivering commercial services that would otherwise be a Mimir Labs engagement (data governance workshops, schema design consulting, ERP migration services, integration services) to referred parties or Participants.

**7.2** Licensor shall not circumvent Licensee by using referred leads to market competing educational courses or programmes.

## 8. Term

This Addendum is effective for the duration of the Agreement and survives termination for any referrals submitted before the termination date (subject to the 12-month referral window).
