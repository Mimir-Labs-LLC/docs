### Article V — Nature of Business

The company is organized for the purpose of engaging in any and all lawful business activities for which a limited liability company may be organized under the Pennsylvania Uniform Limited Liability Company Act of 2016, 15 Pa.C.S. Chapter 88.

The principal business activities of the company include, but are not limited to:

1. Development, licensing, and distribution of enterprise software products, including but not limited to ERP systems, data governance tools, data migration platforms, integration engines, and contract intelligence applications.
2. Consulting and professional services related to enterprise data systems, including data governance facilitation, migration planning, system integration, and implementation support.
3. Software-as-a-service (SaaS) delivery of the company's software products.
4. Any other lawful business activity incidental or related to the foregoing.

### Article VI — Management Structure

The company shall be **member-managed**.

The management and affairs of the company shall be conducted by its members in accordance with the Operating Agreement. Unless otherwise provided in the Operating Agreement, each member shall have authority to bind the company in the ordinary course of business.

### Article VII — Term

The company shall have **perpetual existence** and shall continue until dissolved in accordance with the Operating Agreement or applicable law.

### Article IX — Liability Limitation

No member, manager, or agent of the company shall be personally liable for the debts, obligations, or liabilities of the company, whether arising in contract, tort, or otherwise, solely by reason of being or acting as a member, manager, or agent of the company. This limitation of liability shall be as broad as permitted under 15 Pa.C.S. Section 8922.

### Article X — Indemnification

The company shall indemnify and hold harmless each member, manager, officer, and agent of the company to the fullest extent permitted by the Pennsylvania Uniform Limited Liability Company Act and the Operating Agreement.

### Article XI — Operating Agreement

The rights, duties, and obligations of the members, and the internal affairs of the company, shall be governed by an Operating Agreement, which may be adopted, amended, or restated from time to time by the members. In the event of any conflict between these Articles of Organization and the Operating Agreement, the Operating Agreement shall control to the extent permitted by law.

### Article XII — Amendment

These Articles of Organization may be amended at any time by filing a Certificate of Amendment with the Department of State in accordance with 15 Pa.C.S. Section 8821.1.

### Article XIII — Dissolution

The company may be dissolved by:

1. The written consent of members holding a majority of the membership interests;
2. Entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under 15 Pa.C.S. Section 8972; or
3. Any other event specified in the Operating Agreement or required by law.

Upon dissolution, the company's affairs shall be wound up in accordance with the Operating Agreement and applicable law.

### Notes

- These articles supersede any boilerplate formation documents previously used.
- The Operating Agreement is the controlling governance document for internal affairs and should be executed separately.
- Pennsylvania does not require an LLC to publish notice of formation (unlike some states).
- Annual reports are not required for Pennsylvania LLCs, but a decennial report is due every 10 years from the date of organization.
- The company should maintain a registered office address that is a physical street address in Pennsylvania (P.O. boxes are not acceptable).
